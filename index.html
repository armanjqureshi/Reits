How to read these numbers
  • Distribution per unit (DPU): cash paid per unit for a quarter. SEBI rules require REITs to pay out at least 90% of net distributable cash flow, usually every quarter.
  • Trailing yield: last four quarters of DPU divided by today's price. It reflects what was paid, not what will be paid.
  • Run-rate yield: latest quarter's DPU times four, divided by price.
  • NAV: net asset value per unit from the half-yearly independent valuation. Price below NAV means the market values the units under the appraised worth of the properties net of debt.
  • Occupancy: committed occupancy reported by each REIT. Nexus reports retail leasing occupancy.
  • WALE: weighted average lease expiry, the average years left on leases. Longer means more locked-in rent.
  • LTV: net debt to gross asset value as each REIT defines it. SEBI caps REIT borrowing at 49% of asset value.
  • Share of value: each property's part of the REIT's gross asset value. Where a REIT does not split a property out, the table says n/a.
How REIT payouts and gains are taxed (resident individuals)
  • Dividend: exempt in your hands. The Taxation and Other Laws (Amendment) Act, 2026 keeps it exempt even when the underlying SPV opts for the concessional corporate tax regime.
  • Interest and rent: taxed at your slab rate. TDS may be deducted by the REIT.
  • Repayment of debt: not taxed when received, but it reduces your cost of acquisition.
  • Selling units: held up to 12 months, short-term gains taxed at 20%. Held longer, long-term gains above ₹1.25 lakh a year taxed at 12.5%.
  • Tax rules change and individual situations differ, so confirm with a tax professional before acting.
What changed for REITs in 2026
  • SEBI reclassified REIT units as equity from 1 January 2026, widening mutual fund participation.
  • Bagmane Prime Office REIT listed on 14 May 2026 at ₹103.50 against an issue price of ₹100, the sixth listed REIT.
  • RBI raised the repo rate by 25 bps to 5.50% on 7 October 2026. The next policy review is 2 to 4 December 2026.
  • Q3 FY27 results usually arrive between late January and mid February.
